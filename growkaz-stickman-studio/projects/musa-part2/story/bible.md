# Kisah Nabi Musa – Part 2 (story bible)

**Logline:** Musa, raised in Pharaoh's palace, flees Egypt to Madyan, is called as a messenger at Mount Thur, receives the staff and the shining hand, and returns with Harun to face Pharaoh and his magicians. Tone: epic, reverent, suspenseful; ends on a cliffhanger.

**Depiction rule:** Allah is never depicted. The divine call is shown only as light (pillar of light, rings, god rays).

## Locations
- **Palace** (interiorWarm): lotus columns, torches, gold bands, a great opening onto pyramids at dusk, throne on a dais.
- **City at night**: walls, gate, obelisks, torches, pyramids, moon.
- **Desert** (golden / night / dawn): layered dunes, low sun, blowing sand.
- **Well of Madyan** (golden): stone well with pulley, trough, palms, sheep.
- **Madyan camp** (dusk): striped tents with warm doorways, campfire.
- **Mount Thur / Tuwa valley** (night): starry sky, mountain, fire on the slope, the burning tree, holy light.

## Motifs
Staff = Musa's power (wood, glows gold in the miracles). Gold vs red: truth vs arrogance. Purple = sorcery.

## Music
epic hook → tension (flight) → hopeful (Madyan) → mystery (night, fire) → wonder (call, miracles) → epic (mission) → tension (Pharaoh) → epic cliffhanger.
