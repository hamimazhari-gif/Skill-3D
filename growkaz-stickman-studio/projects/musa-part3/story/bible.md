# Kisah Nabi Musa – Part 3: Lautan Terbelah (story bible)
Same cast, costumes and per-scene colour style ("kisah") as Part 2 (scenes/cast.js and lib are shared via symlinks).
New: Israelite crowd (muted head cloths, no signature colour), Pharaoh's army (guards with spears), horse + chariot, small sorcery serpents (purple), the giant cobra, the Red Sea set (shore, parting walls, corridor, crash, waves).
Rule: Allah is never depicted; divine commands are shown only as light.
Acts: hook at the sea (cold open) → duel in the arena → magicians prostrate → exodus by night & the chase → cornered at the sea → the sea parts (2D + 3D) → the sea closes, Pharaoh drowns → lesson at sunrise.
