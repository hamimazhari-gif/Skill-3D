# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `cozy` (new; round no-sleep face, fringe hair, thick brows, long torso, short legs).
- **New character kit:** `hairStyle: 'hijab'` (headscarf around the face and under the chin, colour `hijab`, optional `brooch`), `hat: 'peci'` (black velvet cap), outfit `batik` (kawung pattern in `print`/`print2`, small collar or `round` neck, buttons, optional red-white `pin`). The crown works on top of the hijab.
- **Cast:** emak (maroon hijab; batik blouse as president, pink flowery daster at home), five ministers (peci, suits, batik, one minister in a blue hijab), Bambang (grey suit, peci, moustache), the chilli seller (tee, cap, towel), the neighbouring seller, a shopper, bapak, the kid.
- **Locations:** palace (hall with columns, red carpet, podium, two waving red-white flags; office corner with desk, three hanging lamps), cabinet room (long table, seated ministers, presentation screen, wall clock, a door), market (chilli stall with 240 chillies and a price board, vegetable stalls, shop fronts), home (sofa, TV, coffee table, wall clock).
- **Seated people:** ministers are lowered behind the table (`seat`); before "duduk tegak" they slump lower and doze, then snap up.
- **Lighting:** warm hall (joyful on "total"; the office darkens when the lamps go out), cool meeting room (tense on "muter", red on "Bambang"), daylight market, warm home (joyful on "rumah").
- **Camera:** calm; wide → close-ups on each line (emak, the minister, the seller, Bambang), macro shots of the budget book, the biscuit tin and the folded bags.
- **SFX:** murmur, boom, cheer, party horn, clock tick, snore, pop, slide whistle, denied buzz, chime, uh-oh, boing, ka-ching, door creak, footsteps, thud, page flips, tin clink, ding, light-switch clicks, paper rustles, sparkle, puff, tada, stamp, chime + click for the comment.
