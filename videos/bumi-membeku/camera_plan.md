# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `cheery` (new; round no-sleep face, side hair, thick brows, slim limbs).
- **Cast:** Bapak (red tee, thick moustache), Ibu (green hijab, batik), Dek (blue tee, small), an old scientist (white shirt, glasses, grey hair).
- **Locations:** space (Earth with frost, a big thermometer; a second Earth with growing ice caps, the Sun, incoming and reflected arrows, a turning cycle ring), Jakarta (Monas, plaza, trees, buildings; snow cover and falling snow), the house (fan, louvred windows, low table with a glowing rice cooker), the rice field (paddy rows that freeze, ice), the nasi uduk stall, the lab (white Snowball Earth; two thermometers).
- **New props:** `monas`, `thermometer` (`set(v)`), `riceCooker`, `fan`, `paddy` (`freeze(u)`), `sunRays`, `arrow`.
- **Lighting:** hot noon grade in Jakarta that turns to `winter` on "anjlok", a cold blue room warmed by the rice cooker glow, a frozen field, a cool lab (danger tint on "bencana").
