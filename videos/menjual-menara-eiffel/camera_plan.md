# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `dapper` (new; round no-sleep face, side-swept hair, thin brows, longer legs). New outfit `suit` (white shirt V, tie, dark lapels, buttons, pocket square), `mustache` ('thin' pencil for Lustig, 'thick' for Poisson and the gendarme), `sash` (blue-white-red official sash with a gold medal).
- **Cast:** Lustig (charcoal suit, bowler; disguised as an official with a top hat and sash), André Poisson (brown suit, bowler, glasses, thick moustache), his wife (bun, buttoned blouse), four other dealers, a second buyer, two gendarmes.
- **Locations:** tower (Champ-de-Mars path, tree rows, lamps, a 32 m stylised Eiffel Tower), cafe (Paris street with terraces, a newspaper kiosk, a cafe awning, the tower far away), office (night room, desk, lamp, forged letter + rubber stamp), hotel (grand room, chandelier, red drapes, meeting table, easel; a window corner for the bribe), home (Poisson's living room), station (platform, steam train KE LUAR NEGERI), police (station facade with a POLISI sign).
- **Lighting:** golden hour at the tower (nostalgic on the rewind, red danger when the police come, sunset joy at the end), bright day street (joyful on "berbinar"), lamp-lit night room, warm hotel (tense on "Rencana Lustig hampir hancur", joyful on "lega"), warm home (tense on "curiga"), grey station, sad grey on "malu".
- **Camera:** calm; 1-3 framings per shot, slow short pushes. Low wide angles so the whole tower shows; macro shots of the newspaper and the stamped letter; close-ups for every reaction.
- **Screen extras:** storyboard titles (cream ink-bordered box at the top, one per scene) and the VHS rewind look on "Kita mundur satu minggu" (⏪⏪ 1 MINGGU SEBELUMNYA); the rest is in-world signs and the word captions.
- **Hook:** in the grand hotel by the window (storyboard), not at the tower; the tower is seen from the Paris street and in shots 11-12.
- **SFX:** suitcase latches, ka-ching, boom, tape rewind (new synth), birds and street murmur, page flips, sparkle, clock tick, stamp, puff, footsteps, boom on RAHASIA, uh-oh, tense music bed (new synth `tense_drone`), riser, a ding for the light bulb, denied buzz, coin drop, tada, steam whistle + train chuffs (new synth), running feet, police whistle, thud, chime + click for the comment.
