## Facts / wording to watch
- Most sources (e.g. Wikipedia, Smithsonian) say Lustig invited **six** scrap-metal dealers to the Hôtel de Crillon; the narration says **five**. The video shows five, following the narration.
- Poisson's wife growing suspicious, the bribe that convinced Poisson, Lustig fleeing abroad (to Vienna) and Poisson too ashamed to go to the police all match the usual telling (Wikipedia, Smithsonian, Mental Floss).
- The second attempt: the second buyer went to the police and Lustig escaped before he was arrested ("nyaris tertangkap" fits).
- "Seminggu" (one week) before the payment is the narration's framing; sources do not give an exact number of days.
