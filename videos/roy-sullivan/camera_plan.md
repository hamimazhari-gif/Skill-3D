# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `sturdy` (new; round no-sleep face, side hair, thick brows, sturdier limbs, long torso).
- **Roy variants:** normal (khaki ranger shirt, green tie, campaign hat `ranger`), `royB` (soot on the face, no eyebrows — singed dots), `royF` (burnt frazzled hair `frazzle`, soot, no hat). Swapped on the strike frame.
- **Cast:** Roy, three friends (red tee + cap, purple hoodie + bun, teal shirt + glasses), a bear prop.
- **Locations:** park (lookout tower, ranger hut, forest, hills), road (truck, dark cloud with angry eyes), town (houses, street lamps), lake (shore, basket of fish, bear), Guinness museum (two burnt hats under glass domes, record plaque).
- **Every strike:** lightning bolt + sparks + smoke at Roy, white screen flash, camera shake, boom + rumble, counter sign "SAMBARAN n/7" and a sign naming the injury.
- **Lighting:** storm look for strikes, sunny park for the intro, grey town when the cloud arrives, warm museum.
