# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `breezy` (new; round no-sleep face, fringe hair, thick brows, long legs, torso +0.13).
- **Cast:** suami (blue shirt, red tie), istri (bun hair, red gingham dress — the same print as grandma's tablecloth), bos (dark suit, moustache), three office staff, abang semangka (orange shirt, towel), mbak (green hijab), teman (red hoodie), the debtor (green tee, glasses), ibu arisan (purple hijab, batik), wedding guest (blue batik), host (peci, batik), bride and groom.
- **Locations:** studio (globe, digital countdown 24:00:00 → 00:00:00, lineup of five), home (door, gingham table, sofa, wall clock; night look for the sofa), office (meeting table, slide, seated staff), market (watermelon stall, cut melon), porch (house, terrace), arisan living room (giant group-chat phone), wedding hall (pelaminan with flower arch, buffet with rendang).
- **Clock motif:** a small yellow digital board with the hour (07.00 … 19.00) pops in on each "jam ..." word.
- **Camera:** calm; slow pushes, hard cuts inside a location on punchlines (wife close-up, tablecloth reveal, WhatsApp close-up, abang close-up, debtor two-shot, host two-shot, bride and groom). Cream dips at every location change.
- **Lip-sync:** every quoted line is spoken by its character (istri, suami, bos, karyawan, mbak, abang, teman, debtor, ibu, host, tamu).
