## Facts / wording to watch
These are well-known retail tactics described in consumer research and the press; they vary by store:
- Bakeries and fresh-bread smells near the entrance (scent marketing raises hunger and spending).
- Staples such as eggs and milk are often placed at the back so shoppers pass other aisles. In many Indonesian
  supermarkets eggs are indeed at the back or by the fresh section, but not everywhere ("sering" in the narration fits).
- Charm prices ending in .900 / .99 (the left-digit effect: 49.900 is read as "40-something").
- Free samples create a feeling of obligation (reciprocity).
- Sweets and chocolate at the checkout queue (impulse buys, often aimed at children).
