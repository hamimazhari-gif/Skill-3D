## Facts / wording to watch
- The story is an illustration, not a survey. The savings stacks in shot 11 are a visual comparison, not real figures.
- For reference: the 2025 provincial minimum wage in Jakarta is about Rp5.4 million, so Rp5 million is slightly below it there,
  while it is well above the minimum wage in Yogyakarta and most small towns. The narration makes no number claims about cities.
