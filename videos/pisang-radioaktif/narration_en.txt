1 | This fruit you eat all the time... is actually radioactive!
2 | Bananas are rich in potassium, a key mineral for your muscles and heart.
3 | A tiny part of that potassium is potassium-forty, which naturally gives off radiation.
4 | It's so famous that scientists have an unofficial term, the "banana equivalent dose", to explain radiation in a way anyone can understand.
5 | A flight of a few hours exposes you to radiation equal to hundreds of bananas.
6 | And a CT scan... tens of thousands of bananas!
7 | So relax.
7 | You'd only get a dangerous dose by eating tens of millions of bananas at once, which is clearly impossible.
8 | Your body also gets rid of extra potassium right away.
9 | Bonus fact: your own body is a little radioactive too, because of that same potassium!
