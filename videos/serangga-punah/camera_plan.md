# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `nimble` (new; round no-sleep face, tuft hair, thin brows, long legs).
- **Cast:** Andi (teal tee; pajamas and nightcap at night), the fruit seller (orange shirt, towel), insects (bee, butterfly, ant, termite, dung beetle, mosquito/midge), animals (bird, frog, lizard, fish).
- **Locations:** space (Earth with orbiting insects), bedroom (bed, mosquito coil, window), garden (flowers, fruitless trees), fruit market (empty stall), cocoa shop (shelves of chocolate that vanish, cacao tree in a pot, coffee mug), pond (lily pads, food-chain block tower), forest (growing leaf piles, dung, green stink), hero stage (podiums, capes, spotlights).
- **New:** insects with flapping wings (`P.bee/butterfly/ant/mosquito/beetle`, `flap(t)`), `chainTower` (labelled blocks that fall when the bottom one goes), `noFace` option for a character lying in bed.
- **Camera:** calm pushes; close cuts to the bee, fruit row, cacao and midge, the animals, the insects on the ground, Andi's scream.
