# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `perky` (new; round no-sleep face, fringe hair, thick brows).
- **Cast:** the kid (SD uniform: white shirt, red shorts; later SMP: navy), Emak (pink hijab, purple batik), the gum (`P.gum`: pink blob with eyes and a mouth that moves when it talks; tourist hat, sunglasses, camera, suitcase).
- **Locations:** home (calendar 2016 → 2023, café table), studio (MITOS / FAKTA), the gut (`stomachSet`: a pink dome with folds and a green acid pool; `gutTube`: a pink tube open on the camera side with travelling squeeze rings; a blockage of 14 gums).
- **Camera:** close-ups for the dialogue; the camera tracks along the tube and the gum is driven to stay in frame.
