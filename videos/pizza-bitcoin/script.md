## Facts / wording to watch
- 22 May 2010, 10,000 BTC for two pizzas, worth about $41 then; posted on the BitcoinTalk forum; the buyer was a young man (Jeremy Sturdivant, 19). Confirmed (Fortune, Bitcoin Wiki, Cointelegraph).
- "About 100,000 BTC on pizzas that year": reported widely (e.g. Fortune, Decrypt), so "dilaporkan" fits.
- "Nggak menyesal": Hanyecz said "I don't regret it" (Cointelegraph interview, 2018).
- "Lebih dari sepuluh triliun rupiah": true while 1 BTC is above about $62,000 (at Rp16,000/$). The value changes every day; it can drop below that.
- No pizza-chain brand is shown (the boxes just say PIZZA).
