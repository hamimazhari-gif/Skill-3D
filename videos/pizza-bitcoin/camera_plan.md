# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `spry` (new; round no-sleep face, tuft hair, thin brows, slim limbs, a little longer legs).
- **Cast:** Laszlo (green tee, jeans, glasses, side hair), the courier (blue tee, yellow cap), a museum guard (cap, moustache), three party guests.
- **Locations:** museum (dark hall, a pedestal with a glass dome, spotlight cone, gold bars, velvet rope), Laszlo's 2010 room (desk, CRT monitor with the forum post, BTC coin pile, door, small table), studio (dark set with a growing bar chart and a rocket; a globe with an orbiting pizza and coin; a second chart that rises and crashes), field (houses, jets, a 240-piece gorengan tower, clouds).
- **Props (new):** `pizza`, `pizzaSlice`, `pizzaBox` (`open(u)`), `glassDome`, `goldBar`, `crtMonitor(lines)`, `barChart(heights)` (`show(u, crash)`), `rocket`, `gorengan(kind)`, `cloud`; a BTC coin texture in `world.js`.
- **Lighting:** dark museum with a warm spotlight; warm room (sad on "kelaparan", joyful on "bahagia" and the party, tense on "tunggu dulu"); dark studio (joyful on "triliun", red danger on "anjlok"); sunny field.
- **Camera:** calm; macro shots of the pizza and the forum post, close-ups for each quote, one crane shot up the gorengan tower into the clouds.
- **SFX:** sparkle, boom, buzz, pops, stomach rumble, coin drop, typing, ding, door creak, footsteps, tada, ka-ching, riser, slide whistles, cheer, frying, party horn, alarm bell, chime + click for the comment.
