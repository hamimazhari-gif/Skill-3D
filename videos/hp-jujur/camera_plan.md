# Camera, lighting and design plan

- **Character design:** `calm` (new; round no-sleep face, side hair, thick brows).
- **Cast:** the young man (grey-blue hoodie), Ibu (gold hijab, purple batik), and the phone itself as a character (`P.talkPhone`: a big smartphone on little legs whose screen shows a face — neutral, angry, sad, dying with X eyes, soft — and opens its mouth on the phone's lines).
- **Locations:** the bedroom at night (bed, lamp, wall clock; blue phone glow), Ibu's kitchen (warm).
- **New props:** `talkPhone`, `selfieGrid`, `cart`.
- **Camera:** a wide two-shot (him and the phone) for the complaints so both and the signs above the phone stay in frame; a top-down shot of him lying in bed for the hook; close-ups for the silence and the call.
