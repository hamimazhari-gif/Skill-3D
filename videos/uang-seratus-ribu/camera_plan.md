# Camera, lighting and VFX plan — Uang seratus ribu

Calm camera (user rule from the previous video): one to three framings per shot, slow short pushes, no dutch angles.
21 framings in total.

## Character design
- New preset `tidy` (round no-sleep face, fringe hair, thin brows, slimmer limbs, torso +0.10 m, legs +0.05 m).
  Added to `DESIGNS` and `DESIGN_HISTORY` (after `stout`).
- Narrator: a banknote character (`makeChar(..., {bill: {color, dark, value}})`): paper body with painted light,
  ink band, cream oval with the same round face (skin colour kept), stick arms and legs, full expression set and lip-sync.
  Red 100.000 (narrator), blue 50.000 (shouts at the flash sale), green 20.000 (travels the kampung), grey 2.000 (parking).
- Cast: owner (light-blue shirt, red tie), barista (cap), parking attendant (orange vest, blue cap), veg seller,
  minibus driver (glasses), food-stall owner (bun), grandma (grey bun, glasses), grandchild (scale 0.62).

## Lighting
ATM: blue first light -> dawn at "six" (joyful on "payday") -> day at seven, sad on "hour". Café: warm interior.
Parking: day, tense on "definitely". Room: cool noon (joyful on "flash", tense on "no", sad on "checked out") ->
night with a desk lamp for the wallet. Kampung: golden hour. Kid's room: warm evening, nostalgic on "rest".
Desk: warm day, mystery on "disappear", joyful for the question.

## VFX / props
Glints (intro, slot, flash sale, checkout, lucky note, piggy slot, disappearing), smoke puffs (note breaks, attendant
appears, note vanishes), espresso steam, confetti (pocket money, comments), floating kiss hearts, shrinking love heart,
fly from the empty wallet, zzz from the piggy bank. New props: ATM booth, scooter, espresso machine, iced coffee, angkot
minibus, vegetable pile, chicken piggy bank, wallet, shoe boxes, notebook.
