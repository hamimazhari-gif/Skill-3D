## Rulebook check
- First word at t=0, premise in the first sentence ✓. Hook ends ~3.3 s ✓.
- 12 shots, one location per shot ✓. Closing question to the viewer ✓ (user's engagement line).
- Pivot / consequence beats: "But that love only lasted an hour" (shot 3), "That night... where did my money go?" (shot 10), lesson (shot 11).

## Facts / wording to watch
- This is a fictional slice-of-life story, not a factual claim. The banknotes in the video are our own cartoon designs
  (colour and value only), not copies of real Rupiah notes.
- In-world labels added by us (not in the narration): coffee price "Rp28.000", money-log lines (coffee 28.000, parking 2.000).
- Shopping-app payment with a cash note is a visual metaphor (the note is "sucked into the phone").

## Timing notes
